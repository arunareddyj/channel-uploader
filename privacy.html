Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Channel Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by its operator to publish videos to YouTube channels the operator owns. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that data is handled.

What the app accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app receives access to that YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, limited to the permissions shown on Google's consent screen:

  • uploading videos and thumbnails;
  • updating video details (title, description, tags, privacy status, publish time).

The app does not request or read email, contacts, files, or any other Google account data.

How the data is used

Access is used only to upload and manage videos created by the operator on the operator's own channels. Video metadata returned by the API (such as a video ID or processing status) is used only to confirm that an upload succeeded.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • The app has no servers, database, analytics or advertising, and sells or shares no data with any third party.
  • No data is used to train machine-learning models.

Revoking access

A channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted when access is no longer needed.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
